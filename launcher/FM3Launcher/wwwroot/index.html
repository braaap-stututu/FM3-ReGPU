About FM3:ReGPU

A native PC port of Forza Motorsport 3 (Xbox 360). The game's PowerPC code is statically recompiled to x64 with XenonRecomp, it runs on its own implementation of the Xbox 360 kernel, and a native Direct3D 12 renderer (ReGPU) replaces the console's GPU.

No game data is included: it plays from your own copy of the disc.

Built the same way as the FM2 and FM4 ReGPU ports, and shares their kernel, renderer and this launcher.

Bindings

Press Bind, then turn, press or pull the control on any device. A pedal pressed on a pedal set makes that the pedal device, a gear put in on a shifter makes that the shifter device, a handbrake pulled on its own device makes that the handbrake device. Esc cancels.
